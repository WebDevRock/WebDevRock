## Notes

- **Delay before retry n** is `base × multiplier^(n−1)`, capped at the maximum delay.
- **Full jitter** waits a random time between zero and that delay, which spreads out clients that failed at the same moment. **Equal jitter** keeps half the delay fixed and randomises the rest.
- **Latest** assumes every attempt runs until its timeout and every wait is the longest possible. This is the number to compare with how long the caller can wait.
- The defaults match [Retry fetch with Exponential Backoff and Jitter](/snippets/ts-fetch-retry-backoff), and "Copy fetchWithRetry options" gives you the options object for it. That snippet always uses a multiplier of 2 and full jitter.
- Switch the units to minutes to plan retries across scheduled runs, as described in [Retry, Back Off, Give Up: Handling Unreliable External APIs](/patterns/handling-unreliable-external-apis).
